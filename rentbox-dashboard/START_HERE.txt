🔌 CONNECTING TO YOUR API:

1. Edit .env file:
   VITE_MOCK_API=false
   VITE_API_BASE_URL=https://api.rentbox.ee

2. Ensure these endpoints exist:
   GET  /api/me/dashboard
   GET  /api/me/bookings
   GET  /api/me/invoices
   POST /api/rentals/:id/rent-again

3. See API_INTEGRATION.md for full details
